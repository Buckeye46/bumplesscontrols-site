The name

Why “Bumpless”

In control systems, a bumpless transfer switches a process from manual to automatic, or from one controller to another, without a disturbance in the output.

It's the standard for every handover on a project: construction to commissioning, and commissioning to operations.

When the switch happens and nobody notices, that's the job done right.
